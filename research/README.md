# Research

`research/` contains experimental methods with no stable CLI or production support promise.
An experiment may move into `stages/` only after its protocol, held-out validation, confidence
bound, and operating limits are documented and accepted.

Current work:

- `data_advisor/`: reusable pairwise decision, multiple-choice, token-stream, and run-recording
  primitives retained from the Stage 12/13 experiments.

Rejected protocols and their final evidence live in `docs/archive/`; they are not runnable
contracts.
